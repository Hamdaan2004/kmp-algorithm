Why it is linear: in the search, i only moves forward (at most n times), and every fall-back of j needs an earlier forward move of j to pay for it, so there are fewer than 2n comparisons. Building F follows the same argument on the pattern, giving O(m). Total O(n + m) time, O(m) extra space.
